Shape New – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy describes how the mobile game Shape New (package com.rednodegames.shapenew), published on Google Play by RedNode Games, handles information.

Information we collect

The game itself does not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Game progress (score, coins) is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process: the device advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data (for example, ad views and clicks), as well as crash and performance diagnostics. This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded ads

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional. It only gives an in-game bonus (continuing the current game after losing).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties other than Google AdMob as described above.

Data deletion

Game progress is stored only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data processed by Google AdMob can be managed in your Google account and Android ad settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
